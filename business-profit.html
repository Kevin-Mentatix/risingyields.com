FAQ

Questions owners ask

What is the Right Fit Meeting?

The Right Fit Meeting is the initial qualification conversation. It determines what the owner wants, what appears to be happening, whether Rising Yields may be able to help, and whether deeper investigation is warranted. It is not the full diagnostic.

What is the Business Yield Assessment?

The Business Yield Assessment is Rising Yields’ paid diagnostic, used when deeper investigation is warranted. It develops a clearer understanding of what the business is producing today, what the owner wants it to produce, what may be constraining performance, where opportunities may exist, and where the business should start.

What is Profit Acceleration?

Profit Acceleration examines where additional economic value may already exist inside the current business: pricing, margins, conversion, customer economics, expenses, waste, productivity, efficiency, retention, operational performance, and resource allocation. Before asking an owner to spend more, Rising Yields looks for what the business may already be leaving on the table.

Is the Profitability Simulation a guarantee?

No. The Profitability Simulation models how changes in key business drivers could affect profit. Simulated outcomes are potential outcomes, not guarantees.
