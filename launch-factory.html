In short

The Launch Factory is Rising Yields’ 90-day business creation process. It takes an idea from concept to launch with profitability, structure, systems, and financial discipline built into the foundation, so the business starts built to work.

What it builds

From idea to launch

  1. Validate the idea.
  2. Clarify the business model.
  3. Build the offer and pricing.
  4. Establish the financial foundation.
  5. Design the operating systems and processes.
  6. Create the go-to-market plan.
  7. Prepare the business to launch with discipline rather than improvisation.

Who it is for

The Launch Factory is for people who want more than help filing an LLC or building a logo.

It is designed for an owner who wants to turn an idea into a functioning business with the economic, operational, and financial foundation thought through before launch.

Questions owners ask

What is The Launch Factory?

The Launch Factory is Rising Yields’ 90-day business creation process designed to take an idea from concept to launch with profitability, structure, systems, and financial discipline built into the foundation.
