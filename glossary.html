Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the business and financial terms owners hear from Rising Yields, and what they mean for the business.

Business Intelligence
At Rising Yields, the lens on what is happening inside the business operationally, strategically, structurally, commercially, and behaviorally: strategy, operations, structure, people, systems, process, capacity, customers, constraints, and opportunities.Rising Yields term
Business Yield Assessment
Rising Yields’ paid diagnostic, used when deeper investigation is warranted. It develops a clearer understanding of what the business is producing today, what the owner wants it to produce instead, what may be constraining performance, where opportunities may exist, and where the business should start. Qualification begins with a Right Fit Meeting. Business & ProfitRising Yields term
Bookkeeping cleanup
Correcting and completing past bookkeeping so the records reflect what actually happened: categorizing transactions, reconciling accounts, and fixing errors. Cleanup often has to come before forecasting or strategy can rely on the numbers. Bookkeeping & Accounting
Reconciliation
Matching the transactions recorded in the books against an outside record, such as a bank or credit card statement, and resolving any differences. Regular reconciliations are one of the basic signs that books can be trusted.
Cash-flow projection
An estimate of the cash expected to come into and go out of a business over a coming period. It helps an owner see timing problems before they happen. A projection is a model, not a guarantee.
Common-size financial statements
Financial statements where each line is shown as a percentage of a base figure, such as revenue on the income statement or total assets on the balance sheet. Percentages make trends and relationships easier to compare over time. Financial Clarity
Customer concentration
How much of a business’s revenue depends on a small number of customers. High concentration can increase risk and affect how transferable a business is. Exit Acceleration
Diagnose before prescribing
Rising Yields’ operating principle. The presenting problem may be the real problem, or only where a deeper problem is showing itself, so Rising Yields looks for evidence before recommending any predetermined answer such as more marketing, lower costs, new software, more employees, or new systems.Rising Yields term
Exit Acceleration
Rising Yields’ work to increase profitability, reduce owner dependency, strengthen systems, and build a more valuable and transferable company, so the owner has options: keep it, grow it, step back, pass it on, or sell it. No valuation or exit outcome is guaranteed. Exit AccelerationRising Yields term
Financial Intelligence
At Rising Yields, the lens on what the numbers support, contradict, quantify, or reveal: historical performance, financial reliability, common-size interpretation, budgets, projections, margins, cash, and resource allocation.Rising Yields term
Gross margin
Revenue minus the direct costs of delivering a product or service, often viewed as a percentage of revenue. It shows how much each sale contributes before overhead.
The Launch Factory
Rising Yields’ 90-day business creation process, designed to take an idea from concept to launch with profitability, structure, systems, and financial discipline built into the foundation. The Launch FactoryRising Yields term
Month-end close
The routine of finishing a month’s bookkeeping: recording all transactions, reconciling accounts, making adjustments, and producing reports, so that month’s numbers are complete and reliable.
Owner dependency
How much a business’s results, relationships, knowledge, and decisions rely on the owner personally. High owner dependency can limit an owner’s time and control and make a business harder to transfer. Read the guide
Profit Acceleration
Rising Yields’ body of work and tools for finding where additional economic value may already exist inside a business, across pricing, margins, conversion, customer economics, expenses, waste, productivity, efficiency, retention, and resource allocation. Business & ProfitRising Yields term
Profitability Simulation
Modeling of how changes in important business drivers could affect profit. Simulated outcomes are potential outcomes, not guarantees.Rising Yields term
Right Fit Meeting
The initial qualification conversation on the Business & Profit side of Rising Yields. It determines what the owner wants, what appears to be happening, whether Rising Yields may be able to help, and whether deeper investigation is warranted. It is not the full diagnostic.Rising Yields term
Scenario analysis
Comparing how a business’s finances could look under different assumptions, such as a price change, a new hire, or a slower season, so an owner can weigh options before committing.
Transferable value
The part of a business’s value that can pass to a new owner, a successor, or a management team without depending on the current owner. Systems, management depth, recurring performance, and financial clarity can all affect it.
