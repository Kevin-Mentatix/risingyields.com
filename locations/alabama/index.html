Alabama's economy mixes old and new industry. Huntsville is a center of rocket, space, and defense work, Mobile has a major port and shipbuilding, and the center and east of the state host automotive assembly plants and the supplier networks around them. Agriculture, especially poultry and cotton, remains a pillar, and Birmingham has long been a hub for steel, banking, and healthcare. Many owner-led businesses are suppliers or subcontractors to bigger employers.

The Alabama landscape

  • Automotive and aerospace plants create deep supplier chains of small manufacturers and service firms.
  • Defense and space work concentrate in a few communities and bring contractor-heavy business models.
  • Agriculture and forestry remain major, and farm-linked businesses live with seasonal and commodity swings.
  • Alabama has its own income tax and a business privilege tax on many entities, which adds to the compliance picture.
