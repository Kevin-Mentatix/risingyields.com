How Rising Yields Works

One business can have an operational problem, a financial problem, a strategic problem — or all three at once.

Rising Yields starts by understanding what you want from the business, then determines what information and expertise are actually needed to move forward.

Start With Rising Yields

In short

Rising Yields starts with a conversation, not a solution. The process moves from an initial inquiry and a Right Fit Meeting through discovery, financial and business assessments, and a road map designed around your business, to a Right Path Meeting, a service agreement, and implementation with ongoing support. Throughout, you remain in control of the decisions affecting your business.

Before we begin

Our Commitment to Your Business

We believe your time is valuable, and we believe ours is too.

When we begin working with a business owner, we're making a commitment to understanding their business, evaluating their circumstances, and helping them identify a realistic path forward.

That takes time, attention, and professional expertise.

Because of that, our diagnostic process is a paid engagement.

But we also believe that if we're going to build a long-term working relationship, the investment you've already made in understanding your business should count toward that relationship.

That's why your diagnostic fee is credited toward your first month of services if you decide to move forward with Rising Yields.

And if you decide not to continue?

You'll still walk away with a clearer understanding of your business, the challenges you're facing, and the opportunities available to you.

We believe that's valuable in itself.

Our process

Starting With Rising Yields

A Different Approach to Building a Better Business

Every business is different, and so is the person behind it.

That's why we don't believe in jumping straight into solutions or assuming that every business owner needs the same approach.

Before we can determine how to help, we need to understand where you are, what you're working toward, and what's standing in your way.

Our process is designed to give both of us the opportunity to get to know each other, understand the bigger picture, and determine a realistic path forward.

And throughout that process, you remain in control of the decisions affecting your business.

  1. 01

    Step 1 · Initial Inquiry

    Let's Connect

    Every relationship starts with a conversation.

    Read moreShow less

    Whether you've found us through a referral, our website, or another connection, the first step is simply reaching out.

    We'll learn a little about what brought you to Rising Yields and help you schedule an introductory meeting.

    There's no need to have everything figured out before contacting us.

    That's part of why we're here.

  2. 02

    Step 2 · Right Fit Meeting

    Getting to Know Each Other

    Before we dive into your business, we want to make sure we're the right fit for one another.

    Read moreShow less

    We'll discuss what brought you here, what you're hoping to accomplish, and what working with Rising Yields could look like.

    We'll also talk about expectations, responsibilities, and whether our approach aligns with what you're looking for.

    We believe a successful working relationship starts with mutual trust and a clear understanding of what each person brings to the table.

    And sometimes, we may not be the right solution. That's okay, too.

    We would rather recognize that early than put someone into a relationship that doesn't serve them.

  3. 03

    Step 3 · Discovery

    Understanding You and Your Business

    This is where we begin looking at the bigger picture.

    Read moreShow less

    We want to understand your business, but we also want to understand the person behind it.

    • Why did you start your business?
    • Did you build your business around the life you wanted, or have you found yourself building your life around the business you have?
    • Where are you today compared to where you thought you'd be?
    • And perhaps most importantly, where do you want to go from here?

    We'll explore your goals, what's working, what's not, and what may have changed along the way.

    We'll also ask you to identify three things that are going extremely well and three things you believe need the most attention.

    From there, we'll look at whether those challenges have always existed or whether something changed that brought you to where you are today.

    Because before we can recommend a path forward, we need to understand how you got here.

  4. 04

    Step 4 · Financial & Business Assessments

    Looking at the Bigger Picture

    Once we understand your perspective, it's time to take a closer look at the business itself.

    Read moreShow less

    This is where we begin connecting what you believe is happening with what the information is actually telling us.

    Depending on the assessment, we'll examine your financial position, business performance, profitability opportunities, and areas that may need attention.

    We'll also explore alignment within the business, including whether the owner's perspective matches what's happening throughout the organization.

    Sometimes the numbers confirm what you've been feeling.

    Other times, they tell a different story.

    Neither is necessarily a bad thing.

    The goal isn't to tell you everything you're doing wrong. It's to give you a clearer understanding of where your business stands today.

  5. 05

    Step 5 · A Plan Designed Around Your Business

    Building Your Road Map

    With a better understanding of your goals, your business, and the challenges you're facing, we'll begin developing a road map.

    Read moreShow less

    This isn't about handing you a generic playbook and expecting you to figure it out.

    It's about identifying what makes sense for your business, your resources, and your ability to implement changes.

    For some businesses, that may mean focusing on profitability and growth.

    For others, it may mean addressing underlying issues, stabilizing operations, or creating more structure before moving forward.

    We'll consider priorities, opportunities, and the realistic steps needed to get from where you are to where you want to be.

    Because the right solution isn't always the biggest one. It's the one that fits.

  6. 06

    Step 6 · Right Path Meeting

    Choosing the Right Path

    Once we've developed our recommendations, we'll sit down together and review them.

    Read moreShow less

    We'll explain what we've found, what we believe your options are, and the outcomes we think each approach could produce.

    We'll also discuss what implementation would require from both you and Rising Yields.

    This is an opportunity to ask questions, challenge assumptions, and make sure the proposed path aligns with what you actually want.

    We're here to provide guidance, education, and recommendations.

    But at the end of the day, it's your business, and you decide the path forward.

  7. 07

    Step 7 · Service Agreement

    Making It Official

    If we agree that moving forward together makes sense, we'll put everything in writing.

    Read moreShow less

    Your service agreement will outline the agreed-upon scope of work, pricing, responsibilities, and expectations.

    We want you to understand exactly what you're receiving and what the working relationship will look like.

    Once the agreement and payment authorization are completed, we can begin implementing the plan.

  8. 08

    Step 8 · Implementation & Ongoing Support

    Putting the Plan Into Action

    This is where the work begins.

    Read moreShow less

    We don't believe a road map does much good if it sits on a shelf.

    We'll work alongside you to help implement the agreed-upon improvements, monitor progress, and address challenges as they arise.

    Our ongoing process includes monthly one-on-one meetings, quarterly reviews, and an annual review to evaluate progress and determine what may need to change.

    We'll also continue meeting internally to assess the work being performed and make sure we're staying aligned with the plan.

    Because businesses change. Circumstances change. And sometimes, the road map needs to change with them.

    Our goal is to help you build something that works, measure the progress you're making, and make adjustments when they're needed.

FAQ

Questions owners ask

What does “diagnose before prescribing” mean?

The presenting problem may be the real problem, or it may only be where a deeper problem is showing itself. Rising Yields looks for evidence before recommending more marketing, lower costs, coaching, bookkeeping, CFO work, new software, more employees, or new systems. We do not promise we already know the answer. We know how to determine where to start.

What if I do not know whether my problem is financial or operational?

That is exactly why the main Rising Yields path exists. You do not need to diagnose the problem before contacting us. We can help clarify what is happening and route the work to the appropriate starting point.

Do I have to use both the business side and the financial side?

No. Each side can stand on its own. Rising Yields determines the appropriate starting point instead of forcing every client through the same path.

What if another specialist is the right answer?

We say so. Rising Yields is built to find the right answer for the owner and the business, not to manufacture a Rising Yields service. When legal, tax, valuation, insurance, technology, marketing, or another specialist is the appropriate path, we can recommend or route accordingly where appropriate.
