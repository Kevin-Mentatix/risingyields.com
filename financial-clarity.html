FAQ

Questions owners ask

What if my books are not ready?

The next step may be cleanup or bookkeeping work before deeper analysis, projections, or strategy depends on numbers that are not reliable enough.

What are common-size financial statements?

Common-size statements express each line as a percentage of a base figure, such as revenue on the income statement or total assets on the balance sheet. That makes trends and relationships easier to read over time. Percentage-based interpretation is part of how Rising Yields helps owners understand what the numbers mean.

Is this a CPA, audit, or tax service?

No. The financial side focuses on reliable information, analysis, planning, and decision support within approved scope. Rising Yields does not present this work as tax, legal, audit, assurance, or investment advice, and routes owners to a qualified specialist when one is needed.

Can I work with the financial side without the business side?

Yes. The financial side can stand on its own. It can also work with the business side when the owner needs both the operating reality and the financial reality considered together.
